Night Float assumptions & boundaries

Three customer cohorts only

  • Cash-in / market and supplier pay
  • Cash-out / notes home
  • Overnight lock-eligible subset

Two machine-checked identities

  • Facility source = unallocated + idle + utilised + repaid + explained loss
  • Customer claims = safeguarded vault balance + explained customer loss

Not established

  • Regulatory or partner approval
  • Lawfulness of customer-supported funding
  • Factual demand, pricing, or loss rates
  • Predictive or machine-learning accuracy

Presenter controls

  • Keys 1–5: jump to acts
  • Space: play/pause
  • [ and ]: step one tick
  • B: baseline comparison emphasis

All people, balances, packets, incidents, prices, and partner states are fictional model data. Customer-supported mode is a hypothesis, not the default product.
