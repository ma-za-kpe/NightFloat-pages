Five objections, answered before the ask

The holes are visible.
That is the point.

01 · “IS THIS DEPOSIT-TAKING?”No. Partner-backed is the default.

Customer lock and fee_share remain an unresolved hypothesis, off the hero tape.

02 · “WHERE IS E-FLOAT AT 11:00?”Consumed e-float has become customer wallet value while drawer notes rise.

The 16:00 rail rebuilds e-value from real notes; no rail means shortfall and freeze.

03 · “WON’T SUPER-AGENTS KILL IT?”They are the dusk rail, not the target of displacement.

The pilot asks them to support physical-cash and settlement operations.

04 · “WHAT STOPS FAKE DEMAND?”Proposed controls: identity cap, device binding, cash-in-belt scope, and score cut.

These are pilot safeguards to validate—not behaviours simulated in v1.

05 · “WHAT IF THE FEE IS TOO HIGH?”Take-up falls.

Empty 06:00 slots, declined rings, and idle allocation stay visible; the model never forces acceptance.
